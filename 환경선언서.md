## 6. Environment Boundaries & Constraints
- **No root**; bind 127.0.0.1 only (no LAN exposure)
- **No CDN** — all frontend assets must stay local (offline localhost)
- **Android Chrome** is the only target browser
- **tmux scrollback disabled** (history-limit 0) — xterm.js owns scrollback; buffer resets on reconnect (accepted)
- **WebGL renderer** may fail on mobile → canvas/DOM fallback required
- **Ligature fonts** not present on device — ligatures effectively unavailable
